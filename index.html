Dr. Anjana Mohandasan

Postdoctoral Researcher in Astrophysics

Hi everyone!, I am a postdoctoral researcher in astrophysics with the ASTRO team at Besançon Observatory (UTINAM Institute) in France. Originally from Kerala in southern India, I earned my PhD in astronomy at the University of Padova in Italy, where I studied photometric binaries in star clusters using high-precision photometry and astrometry from the Hubble Space Telescope. Today, my work centers on low-mass stars, ultracool dwarfs, and brown dwarfs using ESA’s Euclid and Gaia missions, combining spectroscopic characterisation with large-scale survey data as an active member of both consortia. Alongside this, I investigate radial velocity variability among candidate binaries in anomalous populations of massive Galactic globular clusters using high-resolution FLAMES spectroscopy on the VLT. Connecting space-based photometry, near-infrared spectroscopy, and wide surveys to trace substellar objects, binaries, and stellar dynamics, I am also deeply invested in astronomy outreach and building supportive research communities.
