Primary Research Thrusts

Ultracool Dwarfs & Sub-Stellar Science

Focusing on low-mass stars, L/T/Y dwarfs, and sub-stellar objects. Using deep survey data from Euclid and precision astrometry/photometry from Gaia to probe spatial distributions, low-mass IMF limits, and atmospheric evolution near the hydrogen-burning threshold.

Star Clusters & Photometric Binaries

In my doctoral studies, in close collaboration with GALFOR group at the Department of Astronomy, University of Padova, I investigated binary fractions and stellar evolution dynamics in Magellanic Cloud star clusters. The work leveraged high-precision astrometry and photometry based on the Hubble Space Telescope data.

We have carried out a homogeneous analysis of the main-sequence binaries in a total of 159 clusters belonging to the globular and open clusters in the Milky Way and Magellanic Clouds. By accessing the binaries from different environments, we could extend the analysis into a wider range of masses and ages.
