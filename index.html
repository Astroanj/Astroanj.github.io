Dr. Anjana Mohandasan

Postdoctoral Researcher

Exploring the Substellar Boundary & Stellar Populations

Postdoctoral Fellow in Astrophysics

Welcome! I am an astrophysicist focusing on understanding low-mass stars, ultracool dwarfs (UCDs), brown dwarfs, and the complex stellar populations in globular clusters.

My research combines multi-band optical and infrared photometry, spectroscopic analyses, and high-precision astrometry from space missions like Gaia and Euclid.

Key Focus Areas

Ultracool Dwarfs & Brown Dwarfs

Analyzing physical parameters, atmospheric properties, and spatial distributions of objects at the stellar-substellar boundary utilizing high-precision surveys.

Globular Cluster Populations

Investigating multiple stellar populations, chemical abundances, and photometric variations in globular clusters (e.g., M3, 47 Tucanae, M22).

Research Projects

Euclid Survey: Ultracool Dwarfs in Deep Fields

Euclid Consortium Collaboration

Utilizing the exceptional visual and near-infrared depth of the Euclid Space Telescope to discover, classify, and characterize low-mass stars and brown dwarfs across large survey areas.

Homogeneous Photometry of Globular Clusters

Ca-CN-CH-NH Photometric Diagrams

Using specialized wide-field photometric filter sets to disentangle 1st and 2nd generation stellar populations, revealing light-element abundance variations across giant and horizontal branches.
