Films, Languages & Worlds

I love cinema that crosses borders, languages, and moods, from quiet human dramas and visual poetry to sharp thrillers, folklore, and sweeping adventures, in essence anything that is directed well. The titles below are just a small window into a much larger (must) watchlist.

Themes & Special Soft Spots
The Secret Life of Walter Mitty Mad Max: Fury Road The Lake House The Shawshank Redemption Perfect Days The Grand Budapest Hotel Kung Fu Panda :)

Malayalam & Tamil Cinema

Rooted storytelling, subtle humor, and timeless classics—from Oru Cheru Punchiri, Mohiniyattam, Yodha, and Maharaja to many more across Malayalam and Tamil cinema.

Hindi & Bengali Cinema

From Swadesh, Lagaan, Delhi Belly, and Tumbbad to Mira Nair’s The Namesake and Monsoon Wedding, and Satyajit Ray’s Pather Panchali.

Korean & Japanese Cinema

Unforgettable tension, craft, emotion and twists, including Memories of Murder, Oldboy, Parasite, and No Other Choice, alongside Japanese cinema and anime worlds.

French, Chinese & World Cinema

Poetic color and atmosphere—from Three Colours: Blue, The Green Ray (Le Rayon vert), and Amélie to Wong Kar-wai’s In the Mood for Love, Chungking Express, and Happy Together.

Anime & Favorite Directors
Attack on Titan Inuyasha Kamisama Kiss Mira Nair Wes Anderson Wong Kar-wai Satyajit Ray + many more
